# Production Financing
## Payroll Financing

Provide **short-term payroll financing** for local film/TV productions, enabling them to meet cashflow needs for crew and talent payments, while generating fee-based income and strategic leverage for the co-op.

---

## Core Model: Payroll Financing Facility

### How It Works

- The co-op advances funds to cover **local payroll** (crew, cast, vendors) for approved productions.
- Productions repay the co-op from confirmed receivables (e.g., distributor advances, grant tranches, tax rebates, or investor drawdowns).
- The co-op earns interest or service fees, and may negotiate preferred supplier status, **equity options**, or **profit participation**.

---

## 2) Revenue Streams

|Source|Description|
|---|---|
|**Financing Fees**|Flat fee (e.g., 2–5%) or interest (e.g., 8–12% APR) on advanced payroll amounts.|
|**Processing/Admin Fees**|Per-payroll run fee (e.g., $250–$500) for handling calculations, compliance, and disbursements.|
|**Equity/Profit Options**|Optional conversion of fees into equity or backend points in the production.|
|**Preferred Vendor Agreements**|Co-op secures future service contracts (e.g., post, rental, distribution) in exchange for financing.|
|**Training Rebates**|Productions using co-op apprentices or trainees may receive discounted financing, funded by grants or public incentives.|

---

## Eligibility & Risk Controls

### Production Eligibility
- Financing only applies to local crew payroll.
- Must have confirmed receivables or contracted revenue (e.g., grant, MG, tax rebate).
- Must pass a viability review (budget, schedule, legal structure, recoupment plan).

### Risk Mitigation
- **Receivables Assignment**: Co-op is assigned rights to incoming funds (e.g., grant drawdowns, distributor payments).
- **Completion Bond or Insurance**: Required for larger productions.
- **Caps**: Max financing per project (e.g., $100k–$500k); max exposure per year.
- **Reserves**: Co-op maintains a contingency reserve (e.g., 10–15%) for defaults.

---

## 4) Operational Flow

1. **Application & Approval**
    - Production submits budget, financing need, receivables schedule.
    - Co-op reviews and approves based on risk and strategic fit.
2. **Payroll Processing**
    - Co-op either disburses funds directly or partners with a payroll service.
    - Local crew/talent are paid on time, improving reputation and retention.
3. **Repayment**
    - Funds are repaid from incoming revenue (e.g., grant tranche, distributor MG).
    - Optionally, repayment may include equity or backend participation.
4. **Reporting & Compliance**
    - Co-op tracks disbursements, repayments, and impact metrics (e.g., jobs created, local spend).
---

## 5) Strategic Benefits
- **Revenue Generation**: Reliable fee income from short-term financing.
- **Industry Development**: Enables more productions to start and finish on time.
- **Talent Retention**: Ensures local crew are paid promptly, reducing churn.
- **Leverage**: Co-op gains influence over production standards, sustainability, and inclusion.
- **Pipeline Growth**: Financed productions often return for post, distribution, or training partnerships.

## Implementation Plan (First 6–12 Months)

**Phase 1: Design & Setup (Months 1–3)**

- Draft financing policy, risk framework, and legal agreements.
- Identify payroll partners or build internal processing capability.
- Secure initial capital pool (e.g., $500k–$1m from grants, investors, or retained earnings).

**Phase 2: Pilot Projects (Months 4–6)**

- Finance 2–3 local productions with strong receivables.
- Monitor repayment, crew satisfaction, and co-op revenue.

**Phase 3: Scale & Optimize (Months 7–12)**

- Expand to 5–10 productions/year.
- Launch training-linked financing discounts.
- Integrate with co-op’s equity-for-discount and vendor network programs.

---

## 8) Optional Enhancements

- **Green Production Incentives**: Lower fees for productions meeting sustainability standards.
- **Diversity Bonuses**: Rebates for inclusive hiring or co-op apprentices.
- **Bundled Services**: Combine payroll financing with post-production or distribution support.
- **Digital Platform**: Online portal for applications, disbursements, and reporting.
