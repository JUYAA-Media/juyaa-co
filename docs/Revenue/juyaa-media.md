# JUYAA Media

JUYAA Media is the Corporate entity that will engage in the majority of the commercial activity for The Collective. 

## Structure
JUYAA Media will begin as an incorporated Limited Liability Company under full ownership and management of the Collective. 

JUYAA Media will be structured into several departments that will oversee key areas of focus. These department may be incorporated (or become incorporated) entities depending on the 
requirements of the department. 

The initial departments will be:

JUYAA Worldwide
: Global Sales and Distribution for Caribbean motion picture projects

JUYAA Productions
: Shared Production Services for projects produced directly through JUYAA Media programmes or partner productions.

JUYAA Studios
: Financing Fund for Caribbean Motion Picture Productions, managed by JUYAA Media

Caribbean Media Academy
: Non-Profit arm focused on developing training/educational programmes and industry research

## Ownership & Partnerships
- ### Class A Shares (Collective Voting)
    - Held by The JUYAA Creator Collective
    - **Permanent majority voting control (≥51%)**
    - Cannot be diluted or transferred outside the Collective
- ### Class B Shares (Employee Voting)
    - Reserved for JUYAA Media employees
    - Up to **20% of total voting power**
    - Vesting and buyback provisions apply
- ### Class C Shares (Partner/Public Voting)
    - Available to aligned partners and public investors
    - Voting rights, but **capped to ensure Collective retains majority control**
- ### Class D Shares (Investor Non-Voting)
    - For capital investors seeking financial returns
    - **No voting rights**, but full economic participation

## Governance
JUYAA Media will operate with an **independent governance structure** that allows it to manage its own operations and pursue opportunities with agility. However, it will remain **accountable to The JUYAA Creator Collective**, which will retain strategic oversight to ensure alignment with the Collective’s mission and values.

Key features of the structure:

- **Independent Board of Directors**, with a majority appointed by The Collective to safeguard long-term vision and cultural integrity.
- **Employee and investor representation** will be included through designated board seats or observer roles.
- **Major decisions** such as changes to JUYAA Media’s mission, ownership structure, or major transactions will require formal approval from The Collective, using its internal proposal and voting process.
- The JUYAA Media board will function as a [sub-committee](../docs/Governance/subcommittees) of The Collective, streamlining oversight while minimizing governance overhead.

This structure ensures that JUYAA Media can operate effectively in the market while remaining grounded in the values and priorities of the Caribbean creative community.

## Departments
### JUYAA Worldwide
**JUYAA Worldwide** is the international sales, distribution, and market development arm of the JUYAA Media Group. It is purpose-built to amplify the reach of Caribbean motion picture content, connecting regional stories with global audiences and platforms.
#### Mission & Focus
JUYAA Worldwide is named to reflect its core mission: bringing the vibrant, diverse, and authentic narratives of the Caribbean to the world stage. It serves as a bridge between Caribbean creators and international markets, ensuring that the region’s unique cultural expressions are not only seen but celebrated globally.
#### Key Functions
- **Global Sales & Distribution**: Facilitates the licensing, syndication, and sale of Caribbean content to broadcasters, streamers, and distributors across North America, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Latin America.
- **Market Access & Festival Strategy**: Develops tailored strategies to position Caribbean films and series at major international festivals, markets, and showcases.
- **Diaspora Engagement**: Leverages data and partnerships to connect content with Caribbean diaspora communities worldwide, fostering cultural pride and commercial success.
- **Platform Partnerships**: Builds relationships with global streaming services, networks, and digital platforms to secure placement and monetization opportunities for Caribbean productions.
- **Capacity Building**: Works alongside JUYAA Spark participants to prepare them for international pitching, co-production, and distribution negotiations.

### JUYAA Studios
JUYAA Studios is the production financing arm of JUYAA Media, a pioneering venture studio focused on building a sustainable and globally competitive Caribbean Film and Television industry. The fund operates with a slate financing approach, meaning it backs multiple projects simultaneously to diversify risk and maximize creative and financial returns.
#### Key Components of the Fund Strategy
1. **Slate-Based Financing Model**  
    JUYAA Studios supports a curated slate of productions, including:
    - **JUYAASpark Projects**: These are early-stage, high-potential creative concepts developed and produced wholly within JUYAA Media’s ecosystem.
    - **Independent Projects**: External productions that align with JUYAA’s mission and meet its investment criteria.
2. **Creative Executive Development – JUYAAStart**  
    A cornerstone of the fund’s strategy is the **JUYAAStart** program, which aims to cultivate a new class of **Creative Executives**—professionals who bridge the gap between artistic vision and business strategy. This initiative includes:
    - Specialized training in production, financing, and distribution.
    - Direct investment and resource access.
    - Strategic mentorship to empower entrepreneurial talent.
3. **Dual Focus: Creativity + Business Acumen**  
    JUYAA Studios emphasizes a **parallel development model**, where:
    - Creatives focus on storytelling and artistic execution.
    - Creative Executives manage business development, financing, and distribution.
4. **Cultural Impact & Global Reach**  
    The fund is designed not only to support Caribbean stories but also to ensure they resonate globally. By investing in both creative and business infrastructure, JUYAA Studios aims to elevate Caribbean culture on the world stage.
## Programmes
### JUYAASpark
JUYAA Spark is a proposed transformative accelerator programme to be developed through a strategic partnership between JUYAA Media Group, IDB Lab, and The University of the West Indies (The UWI). Its mission is to cultivate a new generation of Creative Executives: visionary leaders who will drive the growth and global success of the Caribbean motion picture industry.
#### Programme Overview
JUYAA Spark blends **post-graduate professional certification** with **hands-on venture studio training**, enabling participants to evolve from creative thinkers into studio builders. The programme is structured into three dynamic phases:

1. **Develop (6 months)**  
    Participants gain foundational knowledge in production, development, and distribution. They learn to shape screen-based content through practical exercises, legal and financial training, and collaboration with CARIMAC students.
2. **Build (6 months)**  
    Teams create studio business plans, develop content slates, and receive mentorship in business operations, fundraising, and project commissioning. This phase emphasizes entrepreneurship and strategic planning.
3. **Pilot (12–18 months)**  
    Selected projects receive funding from the **JUYAA Studios Fund** and move into full production. Participants work with JUYAA Worldwide’s distribution experts, engage with global markets, and premiere their pilot content in select territories.

#### Key Features
- **Studio Creation & Ownership**: Participants build their own studio brands, with JUYAA Media Group holding a majority stake initially, later diluted to empower studio leads and investors.
- **Global Distribution**: Through **JUYAA Worldwide**, projects gain access to international markets, diaspora audiences, and streaming platforms.
- **JUYAA Nexus Platform**: A centralized digital ecosystem for managing production, rights, financing, and distribution.
- **ScreenPulse Analytics**: A proprietary engine offering data-driven insights into market trends, talent metrics, and budget viability.

#### Strategic Partnership with The UWI
The UWI plays a pivotal role as a **senior strategic partner**, contributing to academic co-development, infrastructure support, and governance. The collaboration integrates JUYAA’s curriculum into the **UWIStar programme**, offering students real-world experience and new career pathways in media entrepreneurship, production, and distribution.
#### Strategic Role of JUYAA Worldwide
JUYAA Worldwide plays a pivotal role in the final phase of the JUYAA Spark accelerator, guiding emerging Creative Executives through the process of taking their pilot projects to market. This includes:

- Packaging content for international buyers.
- Navigating rights management and legal frameworks.
- Utilizing the **JUYAA Nexus** platform for streamlined production and distribution.
- Applying **ScreenPulse Analytics** to identify market trends and optimize content positioning.

#### Outcomes & Impact
- Development of **75 projects**, creation of **15 studios**, and production of **15 pilots** over three years.
- Establishment of **5 scalable studios** and **2 breakout ventures**.
- Expansion of Caribbean content exports and strengthening of diaspora engagement.
- Creation of sustainable media ventures led by Caribbean talent.
