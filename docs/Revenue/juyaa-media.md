# JUYAA Media

## Overview
The Collective will form part of the ownership structure of JUYAA Media. It will hold an equity stake in the entity and have a single permanent seat on the board with additional seats based on the size of stake and future structure of the JUYAA Board.

Initial capital raised by The Collective (after operational expenses) will form part of the capital pool for JUYAA Media. This will go to fund primarily non-production operations and investments.

For Production investments such as the JUYAA Start projects, The Collective will be able to determine its participation independently.

JUYAA Media productions will hire crew primarily through The Collective. As membership is voluntary. Should a position need to be filled by a non-member, membership will be offered to that candidate. This will not be mandatory and will be paid at relevant market/negotiated rate.

As equity holders, The Collective will be able to partake in any profits from JUYAA Media operations.

# Project Equity

A Project will have its budget prepared by a Line Producer at market rates. This budget will determine the applicable tier, which in turn sets the corresponding scale rates for the project.

The Prod Co. will sign a Private Placement Memorandum with the Collective which outlines the roles that will be paid at the scale and assigns the relevant equity in the SPV and terms of repayment on principal.

The equity is equivalent in value to the difference in payroll between the market rate amount and the scale amount in the agreement. (e.g. If Market rate payroll is $100 and Scale payroll is $70 then equity value is $30)

Repayment of this value will be on terms with cash investors. An example of Common repayment terms is 120% of principal paid out of “First Dollar” (Gross revenue after distribution & Marketing costs) and the relative share of every dollar after that principal has been repaid.

### Example:

If total investment is $100,000 and equity value of scale agreement is $20,000 then principal repayment will be (20,000*1.2) $24,000.

The project then makes $50,000 after principal repayment then The Collective collects an additional $10,000 (20,000/100000 = 20%)

## Participating Members Distribution

While The Collective holds the equity in the Project SPV, the value of that equity will be distributed among participating members based on the difference between the market rate of their position and the scale agreement rate.  
  
This value will then assign them an number of ordinary shares in The Collective based on the valuation of the shares at the time of the agreement. (e.g. If the Market rate is $100 and the Scale rate is $50 then the member would be assigned $50 worth of ordinary shares in The Collective)

# Dividends

Members will not be paid out on an individual project basis. Instead, they will be paid out on a periodic basis based on annualized profit which is then divided based on individual member’s combined (permanent and ordinary) shareholdings.

# Benefits

## Profit Sharing & Risk Distribution

Through The Collective, members will be able to more directly see the benefits of successful projects without needing to take on the headache of following up on the business side of each project they have worked on. Similarly, efforts on projects that do not see success are seen as building blocks to a larger industry and will see reward as well.

Additionally, The Collective’s stake in JUYAA Media will see all members benefit from investments in the ENTIRE vertical of the film and television industry and not simply one aspect.

## Sustainable and Consistent Production

## By managing a portion of our escalating budgets, members will observe an increase in our ability to produce high-quality work more often. As we gain more control over the financial aspects of production, we will be able to influence the direction of our industry. Viewed as an investor, The Collective will have a voice in production operations, thereby enhancing working conditions for everyone.

## The dividend distribution structure will also ensure more consistent income for members over time, smoothing out the industry's fluctuations and providing greater personal stability for members.

## Working Conditions

Greater say in working conditions and compensation.

## Industry and Community Development

Creating a pool of capital will also allow members to invest through their industry associations in critical industry development initiatives that relied primarily on the whims of external sponsors. Allowing the building of both technical capacity as well as providing a platform to grow the industry.

### Shared Services

The Co-operative may offer critical services to its members, for example:

-          Maintaining an industry job board

-          Maintaining a validated record of Members work history.

-          Tax Preparation services

-          Wage Debt Collection and Enforcement

-          Legal Services (e.g. Contract review)

## Revenue & Fundraising

The Co-operative will utilize a mixture of funding sources to facilitate its operations. The primary source of funding being member contributions and deposits. The next tier will be through retained earnings from revenue sources such as project and subsidiary equity payments and services.  
  
Beyond that the Co-operative will explore offering debt financing for production payrolls. A model that not only creates a sustainable revenue stream for the organization but also provides members with greater income security and gives productions access to affordable, reliable funding to keep projects moving forward.
