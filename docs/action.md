# Startup Plan
Below outlines and tracks the key steps in the formation of The Collective:
- [ ] Steering Committee
Recruit 10 individuals considered industry leaders to form a Steering committee.
- [ ] Call to Action
We will be reaching out to industry members to seek commitments for membership. The target is 100 members who are willing to make a soft commitment of $1000 USD for the first round.
- [ ] Memorandum of Association
The Founding Committee will draft a Memorandum of Association this will outline the key operating principles of the organization.
- [ ] Founding General Meeting
Hybrid meeting with all prospective members to ratify the Memorandum of Association and vote on an initial board of directors.
- [ ] Incorporation of Entity
Founding Committee shall incorporate the entity with the Companies Office & establish accounts for the entity.
- [ ] Registration with DCFS
Initial Board will undertake the process of registering the entity under the relevant acts with the Department of Cooperatives and Friendly Societies.
- [ ] Initial Membership Enrollment
All committed members will begin enrollment and providing their deposits at this stage.
