### Board

The Collective will be overseen by an elected board of directors. This Board should be elected on an annual basis in a staggered manner with a minority of seats being up for election in any calendar year.

A minimum of 5 seats is recommended, however ideally each board seat could be representative of a department or subsector of the film and television industry. For example, a board seat could represent the Art Department and be nominated by a subgroup within the organization.

### Management

It is mandated that the Collective appoint a manager to handle the business and regular operations of the organization. It is recommended that this person be hired and salaried (even on a fractional basis) and possess the necessary skills to manage the operations.

### Accounts and Auditing

It is critical for an organization of this nature to establish clear principles to manage the accounts of the organization. The Board shall appoint a financial officer to oversee all accounting matters as well as appoint an independent auditor to perform an annual audit and valuation of the organization.
